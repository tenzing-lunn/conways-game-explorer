Stamping: Glider

  1. Every cell looks at its 8 neighbours.
  2. A live cell with 2 or 3 live neighbours stays alive. Fewer, and it dies of loneliness; more, and it dies of crowding.
  3. An empty cell with exactly 3 live neighbours becomes alive.

All cells update at once, one generation at a time. The example cell has 3 live neighbours, so it will be born next generation.
